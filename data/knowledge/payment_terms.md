# Finance Payment Terms Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the standard payment-term guidance that the Finance Customer Support Agent may use when responding to customer questions about invoice payment terms.

## Standard Payment Terms

The standard payment term for a customer invoice is 30 calendar days from the invoice date unless a different payment term has been explicitly agreed with the customer and recorded in the applicable customer or commercial agreement.

## Due Date

The invoice due date is the date recorded in the finance system for that invoice.

The Finance Customer Support Agent must use the due date stored in the finance system when determining whether an invoice is overdue.

The agent must not calculate or invent a different due date when an official due date is available in the finance database.

## Overdue Invoices

An invoice is considered overdue when the current date is after the invoice due date and an outstanding balance remains.

An invoice with an outstanding balance that has not yet reached its due date should not be described as overdue.

## Partial Payments

If a customer has made a partial payment, the remaining unpaid amount is considered the outstanding balance.

The Finance Customer Support Agent must use the outstanding amount recorded in the finance system when responding to balance-related questions.

## Payment-Term Exceptions

Some customers may have payment terms different from the standard 30-day term.

The Finance Customer Support Agent must not assume that every customer has 30-day terms if an approved customer-specific payment term is available.

## Response Rule

When responding to a customer about payment terms:

1. Use the finance database for invoice-specific dates and balances.
2. Use approved finance policy documents for general payment-term guidance.
3. Do not invent payment terms, due dates, balances, or policy exceptions.
4. If the available information is insufficient to provide a reliable answer, the response should clearly state that the information cannot be confirmed from the available finance records.
