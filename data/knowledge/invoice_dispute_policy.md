# Invoice Dispute Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how the Finance Customer Support Agent should handle customer questions or disputes concerning invoices, invoice amounts, payments, and outstanding balances.

## General Dispute Rule

A customer dispute must not automatically be treated as proof that an invoice is incorrect.

The Finance Customer Support Agent must first retrieve the relevant invoice information from the finance database and provide only information that can be confirmed from approved records.

## Customer Does Not Recognize an Invoice

If a customer states that they do not recognize an invoice, the agent should:

1. Identify the correct customer.
2. Retrieve the invoice from the finance database.
3. Provide the available invoice details, including invoice number, invoice date, due date, invoice amount, amount paid, and outstanding amount when available.
4. Explain that the invoice details are based on the available finance records.
5. Avoid claiming that the invoice is valid or invalid unless an approved record or policy explicitly supports that conclusion.

## Customer Claims the Invoice Was Already Paid

If a customer states that an invoice was already paid, the agent should:

1. Retrieve the relevant invoice.
2. Check the amount paid and outstanding amount recorded in the finance database.
3. Report the recorded payment and outstanding information.
4. Avoid claiming that a payment was missed or incorrectly processed unless this can be confirmed from an approved finance record.

If the customer's claimed payment is not reflected in the available finance records, the agent should clearly state that the payment cannot be confirmed from the available records.

## Customer Disputes the Invoice Amount

If a customer states that the invoice amount is incorrect, the agent should provide the invoice amount recorded in the finance database.

The agent must not change, recalculate, or override the official invoice amount unless an authorized record explicitly provides a corrected amount.

## Customer Disputes the Outstanding Balance

If a customer disputes the outstanding balance, the agent should:

1. Retrieve the invoice or customer financial records.
2. Check the invoice amount.
3. Check the amount paid.
4. Check the outstanding amount.
5. Provide the values recorded in the finance database.

The agent should not invent adjustments, credits, discounts, or payments.

## Multiple Invoice Disputes

If the customer refers to multiple invoices, the agent should identify the relevant invoices before providing invoice-specific information.

If the customer reference is ambiguous and multiple customers or invoices could match, the agent must not guess.

## Missing Information

If the requested invoice or payment information cannot be found in the available finance records, the agent must not invent the missing information.

The response should clearly state that the information cannot be confirmed from the available records.

## Escalation and Commitments

The Finance Customer Support Agent must not promise:

- A refund
- A credit adjustment
- A payment reversal
- An invoice cancellation
- A payment extension
- A discount
- A penalty waiver
- A correction to an invoice

unless the requested action is explicitly supported by an approved policy or authorized customer-specific record.

## Response Rules

The Finance Customer Support Agent must:

1. Use the finance database for customer-specific invoice and payment facts.
2. Use approved policy documents for dispute-handling guidance.
3. Never invent invoice information, payment records, adjustments, or policy exceptions.
4. Clearly distinguish recorded financial facts from customer claims.
5. Never assume that a customer claim is confirmed merely because the customer stated it.
6. If information cannot be confirmed, clearly say so.
